## Binding instructions, constraints and reasons
- **USD-first and source-qualified:** pricing basis, source and times must be explicit; no unverified current or predictive claims.
- **Local-first Massive ingestion:** reuse owner writer, preserve one-session-per-JSONL, no hidden duplication.
- **Ownership:** no cross-owner writes, no merge, maintain issue/branch lineage and main as authority.
- **Research method:** one incremental feature at a time, preserve negative cases, separate path vs end state and intraday vs completed daily clocks.
- **User interface:** show price×time range, multiple nested windows and participation; compare objectively at fixed prices/bins; avoid visually conflating occupied volume and exact trades.
- **Pattern methodology:** hierarchy of reusable parent/child methods with explicit pointers, not duplicated instructions; capture source artifacts and planned nodes before development.
- **Archiving:** preserve actual work rather than only a summary; commit a clean source branch and permit future branches to pick up purpose records without an active editor.
- **No designated receiver now:** the older B00/_PATTERNS handoff remains evidence, but this capture supplies generic retrieval and owner routing rather than launching a new worker.
