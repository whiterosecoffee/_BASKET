Density assigns whole-bar volume at qualified bar VWAP. It is not exact volume at price or signed buying. Price structure uses two bars on either side of a swing; markers are placed at confirmation time, with the original pivot time in their tooltip. Reversal breaks are candidates, not forecasts. Windows count supplied observations; calendars, missing-session coverage and canonical data qualification remain caller responsibilities.
