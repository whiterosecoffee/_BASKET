Density assigns whole-bar volume at qualified bar VWAP. It is not exact volume at price or signed buying. One selectable past-only smoothed trend is displayed. Adjust 3–90 bars to reveal fine or broad turns. Green rises; red falls. EMA diamonds mark slope switches; movement diamonds mark completed-block direction switches. Swing structure uses confirmed high/low circles. These are distinct observations, not interchangeable confirmations. The readout identifies the latest slope turn, including when it predates the visible window. Flat pauses alone do not count as a reversal. These are slope reversals, not guaranteed durable price reversals. Offscreen portions are clipped. Windows count supplied observations; calendars, missing-session coverage and canonical data qualification remain caller responsibilities.
