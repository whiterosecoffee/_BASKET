## Judgment

The growth opportunity is credible and broader than rented GPUs. The strongest shareholder-profit routes are accepted/billable conversion, renewal of paid-off equipment, and monetizing the operating/software stack without proportionate new asset ownership. The weak link is not a lack of possibleAI use cases; it is how much revenue survives financing, hardware depreciation/refresh, software costs, service obligations and dilution. Platform opportunities do not yet constitute a separately verified SaaS profit pool.

The prior+1197%2028–2030 EPS expansion is retained as arithmetic between unqualified provider panels. It is not a validatedGAAP earnings trajectory. The same provider lists19.59EPS alongside8.5B net income; dividing them implies433.9Mshares, which is not independent forecast dilution evidence. Earlier2x–5x milestone dates remain conditional, not expected stock-return dates.

## Existing revenue base and conversion

The September30 filing has one operating/reportable segment.98% of quarterly/nine-month revenue relates to commitments, including pre-start delivery; this does not mean98% is long-duration GPU rental. Two anonymous customers supplied24% and30% ofQ3revenue. Do not map their identities without proof, and do not mistake many new logos for diversified revenue.

Issuer disclosures distinguish>4.6GW contracted from>1.7GW active, with>1.3GW international contracted power inside the total. Accepted/billable MW is unresolved. Both power disclosures are lower bounds; no precise2.9GW conversion backlog or completebillable forecast is derived. Management quotes20–25M annual revenue/MW on new4–5year contracts and>40M on short-duration opportunities. These are not the fleet realized rate. Site versusITpower, PUE, hardware and contract definitions must match before multiplication. Delivered contracted capacity is assumed fully used in the stress cases; stress is on delivery, pricing/margins and financing, not arbitrary utilization haircuts.

## Partnerships: valuable, with different economic meanings

NVIDIA confirms its2B equity investment at87.20 and a joint ambition for>5GW by2030. It also describes work toward SUNK/MissionControl inclusion in reference architectures and early Rubin/Vera/BlueField adoption. Investment, buildout ambition, reference validation and production deployment are different stages. No exclusivity, residual/trade-in guarantee, price floor or fully funded5GW follows from the announcement. NVIDIA can earn from hardware sales while CRWV shareholder returns still depend on post-interest/depreciation margins.

Cognition's workload benchmark reports up to4.8x total token throughput onRubin versusGB200 for SWE2; this is a specific customer/company performance claim, not4.8x revenue or a fleet-wide guarantee. Faster equipment expands effective compute supply and can depress older equipment pricing even as it unlocks more demand. Serving different workloads across generations may preserve useful economic life, but verify actual cohort renewals.

Google's April networking announcement includes a forthcoming-preview cross-cloud path. CurrentGA scope was not established here. It can reduce migration/network friction, but does not guarantee customer spending; Google also supplies competing compute. Solidigm is a signed prioritySSD supply agreement, not customer revenue. Grafana/CrowdStrike/VAST integrations improve the enterprise operating stack, not automatically contracted sales. Leidos is an intended mission-integration/accreditation channel; DARPA/ParallelWorks is an announced deployed reservation, with contract value undisclosed. OpenAI/Meta are customer-demand anchors with service and counterparty conditions, not an exemption from concentration risk.

## Emerging technology and TAM trajectory

Agentic software, reasoning, reinforcement learning and continuous evaluation expand compute per useful application. Robotics/world models and scientific biology add training/simulation workflows; defense and sovereign deployments add security/residency demand. These do not all become external cloud workloads: edge/on-device and on-prem deployment can limit the direct GPU-rental market, while Omni/software may extend reach.

The IEA2026 outlook projects global data-center electricity from485TWh2025 to950TWh2030, with AI-focused electricity approximately tripling. This is an independent physical-demand anchor, not a dollar cloudTAM or CRWV market-share forecast. Supply bottlenecks remain binding.

The90.3B2030 Street revenue figure versus13.15B2026 guidance midpoint requires6.87x revenue and61.9% annual growth over4years. It needs major delivery/mix/share/monetization success; physical sector expansion alone does not establish it. Power2025–30 and revenue2026–30 are different metrics/horizons. No fabricated precise dollarTAM is supplied, and no company capture share is inferred from them.

Use nested addressable markets: potentialAIapplications → economically funded workloads → reachableexternalcloud/platformspend → signedfinanceablecontracts → accepted/billablecapacity → retainedearnings. Never sum GPUcapex, electricity, cloud revenue, SaaS spend and customers' final application revenue. For a token-billed service,5x volume with a50% unit-price decline yields2.5x revenue; most important is retained gross profit and capital return. That illustrative identity is not the billing basis for all CRWV revenue.
