## Why cash can improve before GAAP EPS

Cohort debt payoff removes its principal and future interest; principal payoff never creates an EPS expense reversal. Depreciation continues until the accounting schedule ends. Cash can exceed accounting profit while depreciation remains, but operating leases, power, repairs, taxes, maintenance/replacement investment and parent obligations still consume it. No new initialGPU acquisition does not mean zero sustaining investment or unrestricted cash.
