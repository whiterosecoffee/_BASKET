# CRWV capacity delivery and backlog conversion

Research retrieved October 22, 2026. USD. B10 #68 / draft PR #69. Distinguish actual milestones from historical management targets and analyst scenarios. Public sources do not provide a complete dated acceptance schedule for all 51 active data centers and all future sites.

## Finding

The retrieved evidence supports an accelerating revenue conversion path: **$12.4–13.2B FY2026 revenue**, **$18.5–19.5B exiting-2026 annualized revenue**, and a plausible analyst FY2027 revenue range of **$24–27B** if additional capacity is accepted on schedule. The old $17–19B exit-2026 target cited in the valuation discussion was superseded by August guidance. October landlord deliveries strengthen the physical execution evidence, but there is no independently verified October companywide billable-power total or newly reconciled current backlog in these sources.

## Backlog conversion: correct numerator and dates

June 10-Q Note 2 discloses $103.7B unsatisfied RPO: **41%** recognized in the 24 months ending June 30, 2028, **39%** in months 25–48, remainder months 49–78. This yields:

| Recognition window | Share | June RPO dollars |
|---|---:|---:|
| July 2026–June 2028 | 41% | $42.517B |
| July 2028–June 2030 | 39% | $40.443B |
| July 2030–December 2032 | 20% | $20.740B |

The furnished investor presentation's **broader $104.2B revenue backlog** uses a rounded 40%/39%/21% chart. Do not substitute that chart's percentages into the 10-Q's $103.7B RPO without identifying the denominator difference. The additional >$25B early-Q3 commitments require their own recognition schedule; none is applied automatically here.

Management also said >50% of June backlog was attached to contracts where delivery had started, expected to reach >two-thirds by year-end. At $104.2B, two-thirds is approximately $69.5B of contracts **with delivery commenced**. It is not $69.5B of 2026 revenue, completed work or cash collected.

## What resolves the valuation

The evidence supports an earnings acceleration in 2027 and a plausible low-to-mid-$30B revenue base in 2028. It does not independently validate the 26% GAAP-like central operating margin or modeled dilution/interest in forward-valuation.md. The valuation remains conditional on those separate inputs. Track acceptance and revenue growth alongside rent/depreciation/interest commencement: a delivered site can initially increase expenses before its full customer contribution arrives. The key 2027 disclosed milestones are Dalton completion, remaining Polaris Forge 1 capacity, first Helios phase-II halls and Bell Sherwood first-phase delivery. Indonesia and Helios phase III chiefly support 2028 onward.
