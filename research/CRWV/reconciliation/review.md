# Review procedure

1. Compare each fact with the named primary filing table. Keep as-of/period distinct.
2. Recompute earnings, cash, debt/principal and lease definitions independently.
3. Refuse positive forward P/E when earnings basis, period or diluted shares differ.
4. Preserve all provider rows; unexplained residuals must survive rendering.
5. Check future/unknown availability and nonfinite/missing inputs. Keep failed
   attempts and corrective retests in VALIDATION.md; do not count test volume as ROI.
6. Inspect live ownership and actual changed files before publication; no other
   owner's tool/workflow/traversal edits. Report actual CI source/workflow SHAs.
7. Forward the specific missing contract to the existing owner. Neither a handoff
   nor this audit establishes receiver acceptance or integrated operational state.
