## Conclusion
SK hynix offers broader AI-memory exposure through DRAM/HBM plus NAND and Solidigm. Sandisk is concentrated exposure to NAND, enterprise SSD adoption and prospective HBF. Broader coverage does not establish superior shareholder returns without matched valuation and normalized earnings.

| Dimension | Sandisk | SK hynix |
|---|---|---|
| Current product exposure | NAND, SSDs and embedded/removable flash | DRAM, HBM, NAND and enterprise SSDs including Solidigm |
| AI mechanism | More flash per deployment, storage for inference context and datasets | Accelerator HBM, server DRAM and NAND storage |
| HBF | Co-developing an open standard; first die tape-out reported; product samples targeted for 2027 | Co-developing the same standard; packaging and memory portfolio provide potential integration advantages |
| Manufacturing | Kioxia partnership is material | Broader DRAM/NAND manufacturing portfolio |
| Key risk | NAND pricing, qualification and margin normalization | HBM competition, customer qualifications, capex and DRAM/NAND cycles |
| Analytical preference | More concentrated NAND/inference upside | Broader AI-memory franchise |

These are qualitative comparisons, not a measured moat score. HBF standardization does not imply shared commercial profits, exclusive rights, a confirmed NVIDIA design win, or material production revenue. HBF complements HBM as a NAND-based capacity tier; it is not interchangeable with DRAM for all latency/write-intensive uses.

## Latest issuer observations
Sandisk FY2027 Q1 ended October 2, 2026, published October 29: revenue USD11.031B; datacenter USD4.802B, 43.53% of revenue, +61% QoQ; GAAP gross margin 85.0%; adjusted gross margin 85.1%. Its presentation attributes about 60% of sequential revenue growth to volume and 40% to pricing. This is growth attribution, not revenue mix.

SK hynix calendar Q3, published October 28: revenue +32% QoQ and +328% YoY; operating margin 80%; net income +216% YoY. These margins are different metrics from Sandisk gross margin and cannot be ranked directly. The issuer says more than half of 2027-2028 production capacity has long-term agreements. No currency conversion used: cross-company absolute USD scale comparison remains unavailable without date-matched KRW/USD.

Sandisk reports an HBF die tape-out and targets product samples in calendar 2027. This does not establish volume production. Both companies released a first open HBF specification in August 2026.

## Falsifiers and next action
Sandisk bullish thesis weakens if datacenter qualifications/volume stall, flash pricing falls faster than cost per bit, HBF slips, or contractual floors fail to support cash returns. SK hynix thesis weakens with lost HBM share, poor new-generation yields, weaker contract pricing or excessive capital intensity.
