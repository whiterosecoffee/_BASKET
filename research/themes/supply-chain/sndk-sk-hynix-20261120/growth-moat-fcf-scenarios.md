## Main assessment
SNDK is cheaper on the modeled recurring trailing earnings basis (~5.4x vs ~6.5x SKHY). SK has broader DRAM/HBM/NAND coverage; Sandisk is concentrated NAND/inference storage with Kioxia manufacturing leverage. Neither conclusion establishes stock return superiority.
Under a central illustrative scenario both roughly double by Dec 2028 and approach triple by Dec 2030. The numbers are conditional, not consensus price targets, guarantees or probabilities. Wide downside exists if pricing and margins reverse.

## Competitive moat (qualitative, not reliability ranking)
| Capability | SNDK | SK hynix |
|---|---|---|
| HBM/DRAM breadth | No HBM/DRAM products; more concentrated NAND sensitivity | Established DRAM/HBM plus NAND/Solidigm, broader AI memory exposure |
| NAND/process economics | CBA/BiCS roadmap and shared Kioxia manufacturing investment | Broad manufacturing portfolio,321-layer eSSD ramp, HBM packaging/process knowledge |
| Qualification and contracts | Issuer NBMs: ~50% FY27 bits and ~two-thirds FY28 bits at Aug13 event; terms matter | Issuer LTAs >50%2027–2028 production,14customers; agreed price ranges reflect market conditions |
| Inference storage/HBF | Enterprise SSD and context-storage expansion; HBF roadmap | SSD/Solidigm plus HBM and shared HBF standard |
| Principal vulnerability | NAND price falls, consumer demand, capex/JV funding, HBF ramp delays | HBM competitor qualifications, yields, large fab commitments, DRAM/NAND prices |

Inference: broader qualified product portfolio gives SK more ways to retain AI spend; SNDK's narrower portfolio can produce more concentrated upside AND downside. Do not infer universal quality/field failure superiority. Standardization does not grant exclusive HBF economics. Contracted bits/capacity is not an unconditional profit guarantee; examine floor pricing, resets, cancellation rights, enforceability, deposits and costs.

## FCF: compare near-aligned latest nine months
SNDK latest three completed quarters through Oct2 (FY26Q3,Q4,FY27Q1): issuer adjusted FCF USD2.417B+5.035B+5.696B = USD13.148B. Revenue USD5.950B+8.965B+11.031B = USD25.946B; adjusted FCF margin50.6745%.
This measure adjusts for Flash Ventures cash activity and removes customer prepayments/deposits. Do not use parent-only capexUSD142m over those quarters to imply wafer fabrication is almost free. Manufacturing/JV economics are also embedded in other cash lines and costs.
SNDK TTM adjusted FCF = FY26USD8.743B - FY26Q1USD0.448B + FY27Q1USD5.696B = USD13.991B. Latest9mo is used in comparison to align time windows approximately, not to manufacture a TTM SK match.

## Relative earnings stress: when does SNDK lose the discount?
Use recurring trailing PE_S5.40295 and PE_K6.48594 with prices fixed.
Equality: PE_S/(1-dS)=PE_K/(1-dK).
Thus dS=1-(PE_S/PE_K)*(1-dK).
| SK EPS decline | SNDK decline at equal PE |
|---|---:|
|0%|16.7%|
|10%|25.0%|
|20%|33.4%|
This is an earnings sensitivity, NOT a NAND price threshold. Revenue=bits*ASP and profit depends on costs/mix/contracts; a17% EPS fall does NOT imply a17% NAND price fall. Margins/leverage may amplify smaller price moves. Uses broader SK financing-normalized6.49x; investment-only6.91x permits a larger SNDK haircut.
If both earnings fall by the SAME percentage, relative PE discount remains unchanged (ignoring taxes/dilution/FX).
