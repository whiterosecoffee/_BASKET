# Ambarella and the DRAM supply squeeze

B19 | 2026-11-25 | USD | Dated research observation

- **Market observation.** AMBA closed at $35.68 on November 25 at 4:00 p.m. ET, down 19.77% from $44.47, and last traded at $35.89 at 7:59 p.m. ET in the cited after-hours snapshot. It fell -35.8% across the November 24–25 regular sessions from $55.56. The November 24 regular session preceded the 4:05 p.m. ET release. This note does not attribute that earlier move to the report. [Historical market snapshot](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amba/history/)
- **Issuer results.** FY2027 Q3 revenue was $115.2 million for the quarter ended October 31, 2026. Management guided FY2027 Q4 (ending January 31, 2027) to $70–75 million. The $72.5 million midpoint implies -37.1% sequential growth versus a typical mid-single-digit sequential decline described on the call, and -35.3% versus the approximately $112 million consensus reported by Investing.com. [SEC exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1280263/000119312526475716/d912526dex991.htm) · [Call transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/11/25/ambarella-amba-q3-2027-earnings-call-transcript/) · [Reported consensus](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/ambarella-downgraded-by-bofa-morgan-stanley-as-memory-squeeze-hits-demand-5050947)
- **Management attribution.** DRAM became harder and more expensive for customers to source, triggering cancellations, deferrals and slower new orders for Ambarella's edge AI processors. Pressure began in consumer IoT and spread across end markets to varying degrees. Management identified capacity reallocation toward data centers as a contributor. [Call transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/11/25/ambarella-amba-q3-2027-earnings-call-transcript/)
- **Recovery and falsification.** Management assumed DRAM conditions would stay around Q4 levels in FY2028, with a weaker first half and a second-half improvement driven by new product ramps. Watch whether delayed orders ship and constrained memory is allocated to those products. A ramp delay or continued cancellations despite better availability would weaken the recovery case. Disclosures do not quantify the split between canceled and delayed orders, supplier-level allocation, or the shortage's share of the equity move. This is direct downstream DRAM evidence. NAND and Sandisk pricing require separate evidence. [Call Q&A](https://earningscalls.dev/transcripts/ambarella-inc_amba_earnings_call_transcript_2026-11-24)

## Follow-up: DRAM versus NAND earnings exposure

### Common market observation

November 25, 2026 regular close, 4:00 p.m. ET, in USD. AMBA $35.68 (-19.77%), SNDK $73.61 (-5.57% from $77.95), and SKHY U.S. ADS $111.14 (-0.75% from $111.98). Each return uses prior close. The SNDK/SKHY price ratio fell from 0.696 to 0.662 (-4.9%). This is one session of relative price action. It cannot isolate causation or validate a rotation rule. [AMBA history](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amba/history/) · [SNDK history](https://ph.investing.com/equities/sandisk-corp-historical-data) · [SKHY history](https://www.stocknear.com/stocks/SKHY/history)

### Supply-chain transmission

- **AMBA.** OEMs use DRAM alongside its processors. Management described OEMs exhausting low-cost memory and choosing between shipping at thin margins or slowing sales. The customer may also prioritize scarce DRAM for higher-value devices. CV7 shipped to three customers in Q3, and management expects higher-ASP CV7/CV8 and CV75/CV72 to drive the FY2028 recovery. Each new product's allocated DRAM, actual shipments and sell-through are more direct checks than a generic memory price index. No supplier attribution or dollar split between delayed and canceled orders was disclosed in the call. [Q3 call](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amba/transcripts/744085-q3-2027/)
- **SKHY.** Sells both DRAM and NAND, including enterprise SSDs. Issuer Q3 growth drivers were server DDR5 and initial HBM4 volume plus 321-layer enterprise SSD expansion. Management reported long-term agreements covering more than 50% of 2027–28 production capacity, with terms reflecting market conditions. The marginal read-through is firmer DRAM pricing and higher-value mix if shipments hold. Edge volume loss and NAND normalization remain exposures. The report does not identify SK hynix as the supplier to the affected AMBA OEMs or establish how much of the constrained memory it sells. [SK hynix Q3 issuer release](https://news.skhynix.com/en/q3-2026-business-results/)
- **SNDK.** NAND has a separate supply and demand balance. FY2027 Q1 ended October 2, 2026. Segment revenue was $5.832B Edge (52.9%), $4.802B Datacenter (43.5%) and $397M Consumer (3.6%). An illustrative 5% Edge revenue loss with the other lines flat would remove $292M or 2.6% of total quarterly revenue. A 10% volume decline requires an 11.1% ASP increase to hold revenue at constant mix. These are sensitivities, not AMBA-linked exposures or margin forecasts. Its latest next-quarter adjusted EPS guide was $55–$57 pre-split ($6.88–$7.13 on the 8-for-1 basis). Its durability depends on NAND pricing, volumes and mix. [Sandisk issuer release](https://www.sandisk.com/company/newsroom/press-releases/2026/2026-10-29-sandisk-reports-fiscal-first-quarter-2027-financial-results)
- **Independent outlook.** TrendForce's November 25 forecast sees DRAM tight in 2027 with moderating sequential price increases. For NAND, it expects capacity and process gains plus Chinese cloud enterprise SSD procurement cuts to push toward oversupply and lower prices. Check whether North American cloud orders offset the cut, and whether NAND bit-supply growth beats bit-demand growth. This is a forecast, against which actual quarterly contract prices and bit shipments should be tested. [TrendForce](https://news.skhynix.com/en/q3-2026-business-results/?trk=article-ssr-frontend-pulse_little-text-block)
- **HBF.** Sandisk and SK hynix jointly released a High Bandwidth Flash specification through OCP. This creates a longer-term inference opportunity. Standardization does not establish revenue, customer qualification, or interchangeability with the working DRAM required by AMBA devices. [Issuer announcement](https://www.sandisk.com/company/newsroom/press-releases/2026/2026-10-29-sandisk-reports-fiscal-first-quarter-2027-financial-results)

### AMBA earnings bridge and valuation hurdles

Q3 ended October 31, 2026. Adjusted gross profit of $68.5M less $57.7M adjusted operating expense produced approximately $10.8M adjusted operating profit. January 2027 quarter guidance midpoints of $72.5M revenue, 59.625% adjusted gross margin and $58.5M adjusted opex imply a $15.3M adjusted operating loss. With $2M net interest/other income, $1.2M tax and 44.7M shares, the modeled adjusted EPS is -$0.32 versus actual Q3 $0.28. Q3 was -$0.29 GAAP with $24.2M of stock compensation. Cash and securities of $282.8M at period-end give a buffer; the cash balance does not change the EPS hurdle. [Issuer release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1280263/000119312526475716/d912526dex991.htm) · [Call](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amba/transcripts/744085-q3-2027/)

FY2027 midpoint revenue is about $396.2M ($323.7M nine months plus $72.5M). If FY2028 H1 totals $150–160M, the H2 quarterly average must exceed roughly $118–123M for annual growth. This H1 range is illustrative. Management expects weaker H1 and a product-led H2 recovery with DRAM conditions roughly unchanged. [Call](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amba/transcripts/744085-q3-2027/)

Illustrative normalized annual GAAP-style sensitivity, USD millions except EPS. Assumptions: 60% adjusted gross margin, $234M adjusted opex, $90M stock compensation allocated across all costs, $5M acquisition-related cost, $8M net interest, 20% taxes on positive income, zero benefit on losses and 44.7M diluted shares. These fixed cost and share inputs are simplified and may rise with sales or dilution. Pretax income = 0.60 × sales − 321. At $400M/$475M/$550M sales, modeled EPS is -$1.81/-$0.81/+$0.16. Pretax break-even is $535M. At $35.68, $1.43 EPS implies 25× and requires about $668M revenue under these inputs ($646M at 30×; $701M at 20×). These reverse hurdles are sensitivities with no probability weighting or target price. Check scheduled issuer releases for sequential AMBA revenue and costs, SKHY server DRAM bit volume and realized prices, and SNDK enterprise versus edge NAND bit shipments and pricing.

### Finished SSD products

SK hynix supplies finished client SSDs (retail Platinum P41 and OEM PQC21) and sells enterprise SSDs. Its U.S. subsidiary Solidigm sells the D5-P5336 high-capacity QLC drive (up to 122 TB) and D7-PS1030 for write-centric/mixed workloads. The co-developed P5530 combines SK hynix NAND with Solidigm controller and firmware. Compare consolidated SKHY with SNDK at the finished enterprise-drive layer as well as the NAND layer, and avoid double counting internal NAND supplied into a finished drive. AMBA's downstream working-DRAM shortage remains a separate exposure. HPE valuation sensitivity: at the $40.82 Nov. 25 closing observation, FY2027 adjusted EPS guidance of $5.05–$5.35 yields 7.85× at the midpoint and GAAP EPS guidance of $3.68–$3.98 yields 10.66× at the midpoint. Illustrative reductions of 15% and 25% from the adjusted midpoint raise the multiple to 9.24× and 10.47× at unchanged price. These are sensitivities, without probabilities. A price drop after raised guidance suggests a repricing of confidence in future earnings, and other factors (including broad market beta) may contribute.

Supply-chain interpretation: HPE is the clearest November case of shipment growth with component-cost and mix pressure. Intel is a mixed case of constrained shipments, demand effects and cash risk. Qualcomm illustrates downstream demand loss with structural share loss alongside it. HPQ and DELL show the same bottleneck does not mechanically imply negative event returns. NVIDIA's November CFO commentary notes supply commitments rose from $279B to $390B, primarily because of multi-year memory procurement. This supports the duration of buyer commitments, while terms and supplier allocations remain unknown. Readthrough for SNDK/SKHY requires realized NAND and DRAM pricing and bit volume; DRAM shortages alone do not establish higher NAND prices.
