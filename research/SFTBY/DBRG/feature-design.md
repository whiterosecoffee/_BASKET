## Economic transmission and invariants
1. Separate parent -> manager equity, LP -> fund economic interests, manager -> fund mandate, and fund/SPV -> project assets.
2. Value attributable manager earnings and investments with their liabilities and rights. Third-party AUM is not SFTBY-owned NAV; consolidation accounting does not make every underlying fund asset wholly attributable to parent shareholders.
3. Fees and potential performance allocations are possible manager economics, not quantified forecasts. Outside capital remains subject to underwriting, mandates, governance, project readiness and investor return requirements.
4. Trace capital committed -> cash funded -> infrastructure delivered -> recognized revenue -> retained profit separately. Fundraising does not become supplier revenue or unrestricted parent liquidity.
5. A shared parent or strategic complement does not establish a purchase contract. Promote a synergy only when named counterparties, agreement status and material terms have evidence.

Counterexamples: a managed fund can reject a parent-sponsored project; investment restrictions can prevent allocation; a disclosed co-investment permits only its attributable interest, not all AUM; infrastructure financed can serve unrelated customers.
