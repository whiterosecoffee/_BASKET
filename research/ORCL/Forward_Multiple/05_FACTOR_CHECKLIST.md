# 05 Factor checklist: what else to consider

Vector: qualitative and structural factors that the multiples and the score do not capture. Each is scored tailwind (+1), neutral (0) or headwind (-1) with one evidence line. The result is a tie-breaker and a risk map. It never enters the Upside Score (vector 03) to avoid double counting.

## Factors (metric, evidence source, scoring rule)
1. Backlog (RPO): size, conversion timing to revenue, customer concentration. Source: filings and transcripts via Quartr, B04. Headwind if conversion is back-loaded and concentrated in few customers; announced does not equal monetized.
2. Capex and financing: capex to revenue, net debt to EBITDA, fixed versus floating, refinancing wall, new issuance. Source: B04, filings. Headwind if funding depends on markets staying open.
3. Supply constraints: GPU supply, power, data-center delivery and energization. Source: transcripts, B10 for the neocloud overlay. Headwind if capacity timing slips versus contracts.
4. Cloud growth: infrastructure growth versus Azure, AWS and Google Cloud, and share trend. Source: filings and Quartr. Tailwind if growing faster off a comparable base.
5. Legacy base durability: database, maintenance and applications annuity. Source: filings. Headwind only on evidence of attrition.
6. Margin and cash path: gross and operating margin trajectory, FCF inflection timing. Source: B04. Tailwind if cash conversion improves while capex normalizes.
7. Estimate revisions: direction and breadth over 30 and 90 days. Source: Quartr.
8. Options and gamma: implied move, gamma exposure and positioning. Source: MenthorQ QUIN and SpotGamma, GEX supplied manually where used. TTD: end of the session.
9. Institutional flows and insiders: holdings changes and insider activity. Source: Nasdaq institutional-portfolio page, filings. TTD: until the next filing cycle.
10. Macro and credit: rates, credit spreads (HYG and the same-tenor ORCL spread where available), energy and inflation. Source: tracker baseline (#75), B17. The tracker's weights are a heuristic, not fitted sensitivities.
11. Customer and vendor circularity: shared exposure to the same AI counterparties across TARGET and peers. Source: B05 relationships.
12. Regulatory and geopolitical: data-center, export, privacy and sovereign-cloud exposure. Source: filings.

## Output
- One line per factor: score (+1 / 0 / -1), evidence, source, data age, TTD, owner.
- Net tilt across factors, and the three factors most likely to flip.

## Failure modes
- Narrative scoring without a dated source: leave the score blank and mark a gap.
- Counting a factor in both the Upside Score and here.
- Reading announced partnerships as revenue.

## Open parameters
- Which factors deserve higher weight than 1 in the net tilt.
- Whether factors 1 and 11 should be combined for ORCL.
