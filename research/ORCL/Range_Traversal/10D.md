# 10D: trailing 10 completed trading sessions

Vector: the structural context behind the last two weeks of trading. Method: `BAND_METHOD.md`, with daily bars setting scale.

## Bar set
- Daily bars for the 10 completed trading sessions for endpoints, range and daily true range; 5-minute bars for the same sessions for touches. Today is shown separately if open and is not counted among the 10.
- Default is trading days. If the user means calendar days, state it and rebuild.
- Adjustment basis: the repo store uses split-adjusted bars (see `history.meta.json` in other tickers). Dividends are not adjusted; say so if a dividend date falls inside the window. Provider records may be revised; print the retrieval time.
- TTD: recompute when a new session's first bar arrives.

## Window-specific measures
1. Band scale: width uses the median daily true range (BAND_METHOD.md); bands here are wider and rarer than TODAY bands.
2. Range expansion or contraction: compare the average true range of the last three sessions to the earlier seven. Report the ratio and direction.
3. Dollar-volume trend: each session's dollar volume (bar aggregate, labeled) versus the 10-session average; flag any session that is a large outlier and what happened that day.
4. Structure by session: highs and lows sequence (higher highs and higher lows, lower highs and lower lows, inside, outside) as a plain list, not a pattern name.
5. Gap list: each overnight gap with size, direction and whether it was filled within the window.
6. Event bars: sessions containing earnings, guidance, financing or partnership items; show the traversal with and without them.
7. Position of the 72H range inside the 10D range, passed to `CROSS_WINDOW.md`.

## Output (three lines)
- Range, position, expansion or contraction ratio.
- Top band with dollar-volume method and the sessions it spans.
- Regime and the largest dollar-volume session with its cause if known.

## Window-specific tests
- Holiday or half-day inside the window.
- Split or adjustment-basis change mid-window.
- One large event session dominating the range (with and without).
- Fewer than 10 sessions of data: report the count and do not pad.

## Open parameters
- Trading days versus calendar days as the default.
- Split between "recent three" and "earlier seven" for the expansion ratio.
- Whether daily bands need a separate minimum touch count.
