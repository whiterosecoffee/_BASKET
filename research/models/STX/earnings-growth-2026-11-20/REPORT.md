## Reported evidence and guidance

[Q1 FY2027 issuer release](https://investors.seagate.com/news/news-details/2026/Seagate-Technology-Reports-Fiscal-First-Quarter-2027-Financial-Results/), reported Oct27 for quarter ended Oct2:
- Revenue 4.233B vs 2.629B: +61.0%.
- Adjusted diluted EPS 7.68 vs 2.61: +194.3%.
- GAAP diluted EPS 7.59 vs 2.43: +212.3%.
- Adjusted gross margin 57.8% vs 40.1%; free cash flow 1.479B.
- Q2 FY2027 company guidance: adjusted EPS 9.20 ±0.20, +19.8% sequential at midpoint; revenue 4.8B ±0.1B. Not a full-year forecast.

The small Q1 GAAP/adjusted gap must not be read as negligible stock compensation: the issuer reconciliation includes 0.33/share SBC addback and -0.46/share income-tax adjustment.

## Growth mechanism and falsification

[Issuer-hosted transcript](https://s24.q4cdn.com/101481333/files/content_files/STX-US-CORRECTED-TRANSCRIPT-Seagate-Technology-Holdings-PlcSTXUS-Q1-2027-Earnings-Call-27-ET-PM-26-10-2026-2026-10-26-0.pdf): demand for cloud storage, higher capacity per drive from HAMR/Mozaic, value-based pricing, stable operating expense and lower interest expense support earnings growth. Management says most nearline supply is allocated through calendar2028; configuration/pricing terms are established through calendar2027, while calendar2028 pricing still needs finalization. Allocation is not fully priced revenue.

The principal uncertainty is durability of pricing and margins. Management says the next gross-margin step is mainly pricing, with external component costs offsetting cost progress. Monitor price/TB, cost/TB, exabytes, manufacturing yield and customer qualifications. Failure to preserve margins or achieve the HAMR ramp would weaken forward EPS. No unsupported stock-return probability or precise fair value follows from these forecasts.
