## Competition and moat
Seagate is the strongest direct technology competitor: March3 issuer release reports Mozaic4+ HAMR up to44TB in volume with two hyperscalers and in-house laser/photonics. Toshiba targets40TB-class MAMR drives in2027; announced target, not verified volume delivery. NAND suppliers compete for storage workloads; HDD/SSD comparison must use workload-specific capacity, latency, power, rack and endurance economics, not only device price.

WD's defensibility: heads/media/firmware manufacturing know-how; areal-density and yield learning; hyperscaler qualification; common architectures; reliability and lower cost/TB. Qualified customers can still source competitors. The moat is more persuasive against a new entrant than against Seagate. Multi-year partnerships improve visibility; cancellation, take-or-pay, repricing and minimum-margin protection remain unverified. Top10 customers64% of latest-quarter revenue remains concentrated; methodology changed to ultimate customers and prior comparison was restated.

Roadmap is issuer-stated, not certified execution: ePMR/UltraSMR and HAMR common-platform approach; HAMR ramp2027 and100TB by2029; ePMR ambition60TB. Higher bandwidth and Dual Pivot aim to preserve usable IO/TB as capacity rises. 2027 platform/API initiative targets customers at200+PB scale; lower-power drives and broader platform adoption can expand reachable demand. Separate recurring software/service revenue, margin, contracts and fees have not been established; no SaaS margin uplift assumed.

AI datasets, video/multimodal outputs, backups, archives and retained agent histories could expand demand; these are application hypotheses, not additive disclosed revenue streams. Not every generated byte is retained, and falling price/TB can offset exabyte growth. Illustrative25% byte growth times10% lower price/TB yields12.5% revenue growth. No audited incremental dollar TAM quantified here.

## Margin and forecast stress
Holding Q2-guided annualized OpEx1.62B, other expense0.06B, tax17%, shares380M:
- FY2027 vendor25.25EPS at21.511B revenue requires61.6% gross margin.
- FY2028 vendor41.46EPS at30.454B requires67.8%.
- FY2029 vendor54.35EPS at36.445B requires72.9%.
These are reverse sensitivities, not impossibility proofs: buybacks, tax and revenue differences can change results. At60% margin, the same fixed assumptions yield24.52/36.24/44.09EPS. AtFY2028 revenue,45%/50%/55%/60%/65% margins yield26.26/29.59/32.92/36.24/39.57EPS. Flat OpEx over several years is favorable and excludes GAAP SBC; revenue shortfalls amplify downside.

Falsifiers: weakening realized price/TB versus cost/TB; inventory growth outrunning accepted demand; qualification/ramp delays or yield problems; customer deferrals; competitor capacity/share gains; diluted share growth despite buybacks; weak cash conversion. Strong exabyte demand alone does not falsify a margin downturn. Compression may reflect lower sustainable margins, risk premium or selling pressure; no causal attribution established.
