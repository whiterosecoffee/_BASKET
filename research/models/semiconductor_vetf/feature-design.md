# Semiconductor research composition
Problem: compare durable future earnings/cash purchased by five listed securities under common regimes without duplicate models or benchmark exposures.
Input contract: source-linked issuer GAAP/adjusted/recurring bridges retaining ordinary SBC/R&D/depreciation; CFO/economic investment bridge; common December2027–2030 per-share wealth; dated ETF holdings; same-session directed bid/ask and cost/tax assumptions. All dates/bases/source revisions explicit; unknown/null is not zero.
Method owner references: #297/PR298 (56e9e03693cfcd71cf0ca04d50470332110defda), #311, #305/PR308 (f4df492838020f82730382752511fb5342dd12a3). This consumer does not fork their engine. Review#312/PR313 (8e3a7c4b327d2abea59bae19fd34e6bce7e79bae) records stale/basis/horizon/cash defects; no executable claim until owner fixes/adopts them.
Output: dated seven-table snapshot, qualified/blocked decisions, 10 unique pairs with both directions, separate deal spread, strategic and tactical weights, prior-snapshot deltas and next action. No private tax lots in GitHub.
Formula: P_i=recurringEPS_i*justifiedPE_i (or qualified segment/FCF valuation when PE fails); W_i=P_i+eligibleDividends_i; R_AB=P_A/P_B; F_AB=W_A/W_B; I=R/F. One W vector per common H/s is mandatory for transitivity. Never fill independent pair fair ratios. If any required W missing, leave affected F/I/threshold dollar amounts null, publish algebra and numeric historical R only. Gates fail closed for unknown/nonpositive W, mixed bases, stale quotes or unavailable rights.
A->B: bidA/askB>=F*(1+h). B->A: askA/bidB<=F/(1+h), h=0,.05,.10. At fixed Bprice pB, upperA=pB*F*(1+h), lowerA=pB*F/(1+h); at fixed Aprice pA, forward maximumB=pA/[F*(1+h)], reverse minimumB=pA*(1+h)/F. For same historical close ratio R, forward requires F<=R/(1+h), reverse requires F>=R*(1+h). These numeric required-F limits are not actual price triggers. Costs: count spread in directed quote once, then fees/slippage beyond spread, incremental tax/settlement/dividend effects in net proceeds. Compare same liquidation tax basis. Maximum extra proportional capital friction for gross relative gain G is1-1/G. Whole-share action requires existing owner cash ledger, not fractional formula alone. Last hypothetical action initially null; no calendar-only trigger.
Regimes: constraints, orderly supply improvement, slower deployment/capex applied simultaneously across every issuer; segment implications in snapshot, numerical outputs blocked pending qualified bridges. Scenario probabilities unavailable. Justified multiples must reflect concentration, durability, growth, FCF and capex; no equal/fixed-multiple default.
Benchmarks: 20%each core; one-third each mutually exclusive economic group (NVDA/AMD compute; AVGO/MRVL ASIC/connectivity; INTC integrated products/foundry), split equally within groups. This attribution simplifies overlapping businesses and is not segment-revenue precision. SMH actual holdings look-through, no corporate ETF EPS. Performance starts at research baseline100; no realized comparative return before next accepted observation.
Review: exact source/branch/paths, EPS basis and shares, FCF period/yield units, dividend rights, transitive algebra and directional boundaries, no staleness promotion, source-linked MRVL transaction states. Do not invent terminal prices to complete a matrix. Tests of algebra are not predictive validation. Prior chat price paths remain historical sensitivities, not qualified recurring models.
