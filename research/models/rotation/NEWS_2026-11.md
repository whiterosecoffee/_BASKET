## What this says about macro vs idiosyncratic
- Biggest single-day shocks (11/05, 11/12, 11/20) were **event- and policy-driven**, and 11/05 happened while yields *fell*.
- A rates-only duration model (`macro.py`, duration ~ 1/FCF yield) explains little over the whole window: 10y +14bp from 11/04 to 11/19 implies about -3.3% for NVDA and -0.7% for SNDK versus actual -27% for both.
- Over 11/05-11/13 (10y +31bp) it implies about -7% for NVDA versus -10% actual, but that span also contains the CEO news, so no clean attribution.
- Non-rate macro channels to model next: equity-risk-premium and political/regulatory risk to AI capex, credit/financing stress (CRWV: negative FCF, heavy interest cost, loan talks), equity supply (Anthropic IPO), and commodity price cycle (NAND).
