## Addressable spend framing
TAM: all global AI services (~$746B forecast 2027); SAM: non-disclosed portion attributable to GDYN geographies, customer size, industries, feasible project delivery, partner channels and competing systems; SOM: actual attainable GDYN revenues dependent on sales conversion, billable specialist teams, reuse and recurring business; **SAM and SOM are unknown**, not invented. $1B annual revenue is only ~0.134% of broad global 2027 market and is GDYN's own aspirational GigaCube milestone, not a forecast.

## Repurposing vs legacy drag
The non-AI services base is not intrinsically obsolete; could be retrained toward solution architecture, governance, inference economics, agent operations, robotics simulation, high-value data migration and recurring managed optimization. Revenue attribution should not double count converted accounts as net-new TAM. Track cohort-level sales per client, AI mix, revenue per engineer, billable utilization, productized IP reuse, incremental gross margin, recurring run rate, retraining costs, delivery hours eliminated, price per project and client concentration.

## Potential revenue timeline — assumption only
FY2026 anchor $441.058m derived from issuer 9M revenue plus Q4 midpoint. To attain $1B: 2027 +25% then 2028 +30% then 2029 +25% then 2030 +12% produces ~ $1.005B (441.058*1.25*1.30*1.25*1.12=1,003.5m). Requires substantial sales execution, margin/GAAP-to-cash conversion and possibly acquisition contribution. A 30% compound growth rate yields $968.5m FY2029 and $1.26B FY2030; no proof company can maintain it. Avoid using 31% * 45% and 69% * other growth on *current* mix as an exact prospective growth attribution: must use beginning-of-period AI mix; customer category definitions and mix shifts matter.

## Falsifiers
High-level TAM growth does not translate to GDYN share unless enterprise spending actually flows to specialist integrators; incumbent platforms can displace custom work. Reusable tooling can reduce billable hours faster than value-based pricing compensates. AI agents lower barriers to entry. Physical AI deployment has longer sales/acceptance cycles. Track Q4 deceleration, organic versus acquired growth, sales productivity, deferred revenue/backlog, customer concentration, sustainable GAAP EPS and operating cash flow versus adjusted EPS.
