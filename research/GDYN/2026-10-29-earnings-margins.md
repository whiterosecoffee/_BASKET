# GDYN — Q3 2026 earnings and margins

Issuer: Grid Dynamics Holdings, Inc. (NASDAQ: GDYN). Reporting period ended 2026-09-30; release 2026-10-29. Currency USD. Source class: company-reported GAAP, company-reported non-GAAP and derived computation. Not a live market record.

## Operating evidence
- Revenue $114.3 million, up 9.7% YoY; Q3 guidance had been $112–114 million.
- AI revenue 31% of total (implied approximately $35.43m). Issuer says AI revenue expanded more than 45% YoY for three successive quarters; AI accounting definition and economics need continued monitoring.
- Technology/media/telecom 32.3% of revenue, +29.3% YoY; retail 27.2%, +13.3% YoY; finance 22.5%.
- GAAP gross margin 40.3%, versus 33.3% in Q3 2025, +7.0 percentage points.
- Adjusted EBITDA $22.9m (~20.0% revenue), versus $12.7m (~12.2%) in Q3 2025.
- Adjusted EPS $0.18 versus $0.09; GAAP net income $6.2m versus $1.2m. Non-GAAP EPS is not equivalent to free cash flow.
- Nine-month operating cash flow approximately $30.5m, roughly flat YoY; cash approximately $304m.
- Q4 revenue guidance $113.5m–$115.5m and adjusted EBITDA $20m–$22m: implied moderation from Q3 and no basis for blindly annualizing Q3 margins.

## Margin bridge and qualitative opportunities
At $114.3m of sales, gross margin +7.0pp corresponds to approximately $8.0m more quarterly gross profit compared with maintaining the 2025 margin on the current sales base (114.3 × .07). Hypothesized drivers: AI-accelerated software delivery, reuse of GAIN agents/solution accelerators, engineer utilization, cost control and higher-specialization engagements. These individual drivers are *not* quantified by GAAP segment margins; no claim of SaaS margins or separately reported NVIDIA-derived revenue.

## Falsifiers
- Q4 profitability fails to stabilize; AI revenue share increases while consolidated growth stalls.
- GAAP earnings and operating cash flow fail to corroborate adjusted EPS expansion.
- Premium service pricing or reusable IP fails to improve durable delivery contribution margins.
