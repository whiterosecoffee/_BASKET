## Resource model / differentiation from power
Power is constrained in physical MW, lead-time and rights-of-use. Implementation throughput is constrained by **effective specialist team capacity**: teams × deployable skill fraction × acceptance rate × projects/team/year, multiplied by output delivered per trained team and reusable tooling. It is not a fixed ceiling: AI tooling may improve throughput, training/hiring may enlarge capacity; model uncertainty explicitly. Actual monetizable specialist capacity requires evidence of billable utilization, project backlog, lead times, revenue per specialist, gross profit per project and client retention. Do not invent engineer counts, labor-rate uplift or scarcity premiums.

## Economic hypothesis / falsifiers
If enterprise AI demand outruns production-ready engineering and GDYN proves reusability, project prices/mix, conversion per employee, and gross profit per deployment can rise faster than headcount. However automation can also compress billable hours and commoditize service pricing; enterprise incumbents and platforms may internalize deployments. Q4 2026 guidance near flat sequential revenue is current counterevidence against immediate broad acceleration. Test organic AI growth vs adjusted/GAAP margin bridge, operating cash flow, deployment repeat-rate, backlog, training costs and employee churn.
