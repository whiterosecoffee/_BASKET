# AAOI integration and moat assessment
As of November 20, 2026. Fundamental research, no fresh quote or canonical ingestion.

Assessment (inference): credible manufacturing/process advantage and customer qualification barriers; a durable wide economic moat is not yet demonstrated. Integration can increase earnings through cost/yield control, but requires capital and does not establish pricing power.

Company evidence:
- In-house semiconductor/laser production using MBE and MOCVD; manufacturing through components and transceivers. Source https://newsroom.ao-inc.com/assets/at-a-glance/AOI-At-a-Glance.pdf . Brochure is undated; its old capacity/market-cap statistics are not used as current.
- Q3 filing describes conditional three-year hyperscaler sourcing agreement for 10% of specified 1.6T requirements, June2026–May2029, with maximum pricing and supplier capacity obligations. This supports customer embedding, not exclusivity or fixed-dollar guaranteed backlog. Customer identity not inferred. Source https://appliedoptoelectronics.gcs-web.com/node/17736/html .
- November5 release: equipment capacity approaching330k/month, targets >500k end2026 and >1M end2027; US power/construction delays. Installed capacity is not actual saleable output. Source https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1158114/000168316826008351/aaoi_ex9901.htm .
- March9 first 1.6T volume order: https://newsroom.ao-inc.com/news-releases/aoi-receives-first-volume-order-of-1-6t-data-center-transceivers-from-major-hyperscale-customer/ .
- March17 ELSFP portfolio aimed at silicon photonics/NPO/CPO: https://investors.ao-inc.com/2026-03-17-AOI-Showcases-25dBm-Ultra-High-Power-ELSFP-as-the-Foundation-for-Next-Gen-AI-Infrastructure-at-OFC-2026 . Demonstration is not verified material volume revenue.

Growth logic: qualified units times ASP -> revenue; higher yield/automation and utilization -> unit cost and margins; cash earnings less growth investment -> financing need; income divided by weighted diluted shares -> shareholder benefit. No independent quantified unit-cost advantage or comparative competitor benchmark established.

CPO offers a possible laser-supply route if module architecture changes, while risking displacement of existing pluggable module value. Do not simply add both markets without cannibalization/share assumptions. Integration does not eliminate external component dependency or customer pricing pressure.

Falsifiers and next evidence: saleable qualified output vs equipment capacity, gross profit per unit after ASP declines, customer diversification, cash conversion, capex efficiency and diluted EPS growth. A growing industry and output capacity alone do not validate +435.7% forecast EPS growth or an automatic rerating. Next action remains verify FY2027 share denominator and actual ATM issuance, then source a quarterly shipment/yield/margin bridge. This note postdates archived packet; reconcile on next pickup.
