# CPO product scope
Checked November20 2026. Correction/extension: AAOI public portfolio includes more than lasers: 6.4T on-board optics powered by its ELSFP were showcased at OFC2026. On-board optics are distinct from co-packaging optical engines with switching silicon. No complete AAOI switch-ASIC plus co-packaged engines plus system/software product verified.
Source: https://newsroom.ao-inc.com/latest-news/the-future-of-ai-data-center-networking-cpo-xpo-and-optical-innovation/ .
Source Photonics offers/demonstrates transceivers, ELSFP and 6.4/12.8T XPO. XPO remains pluggable and is not CPO. https://www.sourcephotonics.com/news/source-photonics-to-spotlight-its-latest-optical-innovations-announce-to-receive-two-industry-awards-for-two-product-families-during-ofc26/ .
NVIDIA announced Spectrum-X/Quantum-X photonics integrated networking switches March18 2025, naming Coherent/Lumentum and others in ecosystem; AAOI not named in that announcement, absence not proof no business. https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-spectrum-x-co-packaged-optics-networking-switches-ai-factories/ . Announcement's availability is planned at that date, not separately verified current shipment status.
Ayar Labs describes optical engine TeraPHY plus SuperNova external light source as complete CPO solution for integration into host chips/packages, not a complete GPU/server/switch platform. https://ayarlabs.com/products/ .
Inference: AAOI positioned at optical components/subsystems, with OBO demonstrating broader integration potential; no evidence of full-system economics. ELSFP can remain external/serviceable while engines are co-packaged. TAM capture must reflect actual supplied content, qualification, margins and optical architecture substitution rather than entire switch value. No price refresh or canonical ingestion performed.
