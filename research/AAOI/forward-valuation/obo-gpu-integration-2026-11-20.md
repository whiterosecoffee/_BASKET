# Can AAOI 6.4T OBO run inside a GPU?
Research November20 2026. Sources: https://ao-inc.com/products/optical-transceivers/ and https://newsroom.ao-inc.com/news-releases/aoi-showcases-25dbm-ultra-high-power-elsfp-as-the-foundation-for-next-gen-ai-infrastructure-at-ofc-2026/ . AAOI describes a live6.4T on-board optics demo powered by400mW external ELSFP and positions engine close to switch ASIC in102.4T+ switching architectures. No AAOI GPU-package optical chiplet or named GPU design win verified.

Engineering interpretation: OBO is board-level optical I/O, converting electrical data to/from light. It does not execute CUDA, matrix arithmetic or replace GPU compute/HBM. GPU board use is conceptually feasible only with compatible electrical/protocol interfaces, power/thermal design and qualification; this demo does not prove drop-in GPU compatibility. In-package optical I/O requires chiplet interfaces, package integration, fiber attachment, thermal/reliability validation and host vendor co-design. AAOI ELS could potentially illuminate a compatible third-party engine without AAOI engine being in-package; no such customer contract inferred.

Ayar Labs explicitly describes TeraPHY/SuperNova in-package optical I/O forGPU/CPU/accelerators: https://ayarlabs.com/teraphY/ and validation https://ayarlabs.com/blog/lets-get-serious-teraphy-optical-engine-passes-the-test-for-ai-scale-up-at-volume . Vendor claims/preliminary specifications, not proof of shipment in a named production NVIDIA GPU. Illustrates architecture feasibility, not AAOI implementation.

6.4Tb/s =0.8TB/s raw aggregate arithmetic, not compute throughput or necessarily one-direction payload throughput; public pages do not establish detailed lane/direction/overhead accounting. Do not compare directly with HBM bandwidth without normalization.

Opportunity: AAOI may move from lasers to modules and board optical engines, increasing delivered content; eventual GPU-package engine opportunity is an additional unverified step. Watch complete electrical interface, pJ/bit, latency/FEC, thermal spec, customer qualification and production revenue. Roadmap search mentions planned Q4 2026 NPO/OBO mass production, but presentation timing may precede later delays; no achieved production claim made. No current quote or canonical ingestion.
