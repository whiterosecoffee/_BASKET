# AI services differences

Roles: GDYN digital engineering and data/platform implementation; EXLS domain
operations and analytics/data/AI services; G business-process transformation,
Advanced Technology Solutions (ATS) and Core Business Services (CBS); GIB broad
IT consulting, integration and managed services. Roles overlap.

GDYN/EXLS/G use calendar years. CGI fiscal year ends September: September2026
is FY26 Q4. Quarterly end dates align here; yearly EPS labels do not.

Annualized labor proxy = quarterly revenue * 4 / exact period-end employees.
Use matched dates; no rounded website counts. This is not TTM revenue / average
headcount or proof of AI productivity. Acquisition scope, geography, subcontracting,
pricing and mix are confounders.

CGI USD display = original CAD / FXUSDCAD. Both comparison periods use the
November18 Bank of Canada reference rate; not historical-period-average USD.
Keep margins distinct: GDYN adjusted EBITDA, EXLS/G adjusted operating, CGI EBIT.

EPS gate: verify fiscal end, share class/dilution, accounting basis, provider
vintage and coverage. No 2027/2028 ranking before compatible estimates and USD
price/FX. Cached search/page copies are not successive forecast revisions.
