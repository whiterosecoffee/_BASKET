# Review the smallest completed change

1. Verify exact issuer value, unit, period and accounting basis.
2. Compare last published commit; distinguish period growth from estimate revision.
3. Replay twice; preserve history and missing reasons.
4. Check FX direction, share basis, fiscal calendar and margin definitions.
5. Verify exclusive paths and private-data exclusion.
6. Record local tests, remote head, CI and independent review separately.
7. Name the evidence gap most likely to change interpretation.

Initial source review covers four issuer releases and official FX. There is no
prior persisted baseline. Forecast leads remain quarantined until basis/vintage
are reconciled. Tests are recorded after execution in the issue resolution.
