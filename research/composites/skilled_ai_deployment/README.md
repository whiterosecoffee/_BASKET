# Skilled AI Deployment & Enterprise Integration Basket

Object: [basket.json](basket.json), `basket.skilled_ai_deployment@1-provisional` (not an approved shared schema). Issue #118; B17 research scope. Contains 24 venue-qualified public securities across specialist, global integrator, offshore engineering, and platform-led categories. This is an **AI deployment skill scarcity** basket, not an AI power/compute basket or buy list.

Selection lens: proven specialist engineering and domain transformation, reusable delivery IP, production implementation, trained teams, and ability to earn pricing power. Missing per-firm scores are null; membership is research candidacy, not proof of scarce proprietary skill or profitable AI growth. Cross-company comparisons belong in this composite, not company folders. `GDYN` company research remains standalone.

Venues: `NSE_INDIA` tickers are India listed, `EURONEXT_PARIS` is France, `OTC_US` is OTC ADR. Avoid treating them as U.S. Nasdaq securities. USD comparability needs sourced FX.

Dependencies: B00 must approve a canonical reusable **basket object type** and registration before publication as system-supported; B01 owns integration validation; B02 owns quote/history ingestion. No shared schema, price history or existing B17 market-context files were changed. Source links and qualification caveats are embedded in JSON. No independent CI verified.
