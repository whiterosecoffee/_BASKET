Five-fiscal-step adjusted EPS CAGR: 42.5%.

Basis: adjusted/normalized EPS; this is not a GAAP forecast. ChartMill displays this as an analyst forecast series. Exact annual panel counts and table timestamp were not verified. FY27 estimate-history last observation is November 14. S&P Global via Stock Analysis gives $2.27/$3.09 for FY27/FY28 versus $2.28/$3.10 here. Other aggregators differ more at distant horizons. YoY rates above are recomputed from rounded EPS, not copied vendor percentages.

The acceleration is back-loaded: roughly 29%, 36% and 34% annual EPS growth is followed by 49% and 68%. At the reference price, the adjusted multiple remains about 37x March 2029 earnings and only approaches 25x by March 2030. Those are estimates, not commitments.

Arm separately targets more than $9 adjusted EPS in FYE31; the $10.40 figure is this third-party forecast, not the issuer target and not GAAP EPS. Existing GAAP consensus-derived references are around $1.75 for March 2028 and $2.60 for March 2029 under our assumed diluted shares. No GAAP FY30/FY31 EPS forecast was independently established.

FY27 is already in progress; March 2031 is about 4.36 years from November 20, 2026. A holding period is not an earnings-payback period. EPS is not a cash distribution, and investment returns depend on valuation, execution, ownership and distributions.
