## Incremental economics and falsifiers
Illustrative after-cost operating contribution80% for extra IP/royalty revenue versus20–30% for silicon, tax15%, diluted shares1.2B: extra$1B annual revenue contributes about$0.57 EPS for IP versus$0.14–0.21 for chips. These are analyst-selected sensitivities retaining costs in the contribution margin, not reported ARM partner margins. Do not add them to a consensus already containing that growth.

Bull evidence: NVIDIA Arm-compatible CPU adoption outside accelerator-attached racks; faster CSS royalty content and data-center shipment growth; new direct silicon customers; first deliveries Jan–Mar2027; manufacturing margin improvement; recurring costs/diluted shares growing slower than gross profit.
Bear evidence: licensing fails to convert to shipment royalties, CPU efficiency reduces unit requirements, custom architectures carry lower realized fees, direct silicon competes with licensees, R&D/SBC or supply costs absorb growth. Co-packaging benefits ARM only where it increases monetized Arm IP/processor content; no automatic entitlement to optics/interconnect spending.
