# ARM revenue streams, market expansion and earnings stress test

As of November 20, 2026. USD. FY2031 ends March 31, 2031. Scenario horizon about 4.36 years. Same reference close $152.20; no live quote feed.

The growth mechanisms are identifiable; the investment question is realized profit per share after development, manufacturing, stock compensation and dilution. Consensus already includes significant expansion; undisclosed incremental upside has not been established.

## Revenue routes and partner evidence

| Revenue route | Economics | Partner/evidence status |
|---|---|---|
| Royalties on customer chips | Contractual fee per chip or percentage of chip ASP; richer IP/CSS can raise content. Customer cloud revenue and capex are not ARM revenue. | AWS Graviton, Google Axion, Microsoft Cobalt, NVIDIA Vera/BlueField; customer designs can also compete with ARM direct silicon. Established; datacenter growth strong but consolidated royalty growth only 8% in latest quarter. [results] |
| License and other | Design-access/subscription and delivery-linked recognition; not an independent annuity on top of royalties. | Semiconductor, hyperscaler and OEM licensees; counterparty amounts undisclosed. Established, lumpy, +50% latest quarter; monitor ACV/RPO and subsequent royalties. [results] |
| Direct AGI CPU sales | Full chip revenue less manufacturing/packaging, engineering, selling and compensation. No extra internal third-party royalty. | Meta multigeneration co-development independently confirmed; OCI planned fall 2027; OpenAI, SAP, Cloudflare and Cerebras in announced ecosystem, without verified individual order dollars. Multiple-customer purchase orders reported; first commercial shipments planned Jan–Mar 2027. [meta] |
| Mobile/PC and local AI | Armv9, CSS for Mobile 2, SME2 and GPU capability increase licensable content; hardware performance is not automatically a higher contracted fee. | OPPO, vivo, Xiaomi, Google; KleidiAI/software framework integration supports adoption. Edge royalties roughly flat despite technology expansion; no separate software SaaS revenue assumed. [results] |
| Automotive/robotics/industrial | Zena CSS, safety/real-time CPU content and richer silicon per vehicle/machine; long design/validation cycles. | Tensor, NXP, Siemens, AWS and Unitree ecosystem participation; 80+ partners do not establish purchase orders. Physical AI royalties +50%; robotics revenue and timing not separately quantified. [physical] |

Latest Q2 FY2027: royalties $670M (+8%); license/other $773M (+50%). Datacenter royalties doubled and physical AI royalties grew 50%, while edge was roughly flat. Licensing is an earlier design cohort; growth will transmit only when products ship under monetizable terms. No exact segment-weighted royalty bridge is available. [results, november_call]

## TAM and timing

Data-center CPU opportunity: issuer estimate above $100B by 2030. The $15B FY2031 direct-chip objective would represent roughly 15% of $100B, with a fiscal/calendar timing mismatch. It is an aggressive share objective, not a royalty entitlement. Edge TAM +40% over five years is only about 7% annual growth. Physical AI compute is a management-estimated $200B annual opportunity in the 2030s, without an exact year. These measures overlap and cannot be added into Arm revenue. [long_term, physical]

Near-term CPU outlook is up to $2B across FY2027 AND FY2028, versus $15B in FY2031 alone. Even granting all $2B to FY2028 for illustration requires about 96% annual chip growth over the following three years. Purchase orders are reported but individual order dollars, acceptance and cancellation terms are not verified. [results]

## Emerging technologies and expansion

CSS N4 enables configurable custom CPUs, networking, security and DPUs, with LPDDR6/PCIe Gen7 and higher memory bandwidth. The economic opportunity is richer IP content and quicker deployment, not automatic payment per core. [n4]

Armv9, SME2, Mobile CSS 2 and new GPU IP expand on-device agent/inference capability across phones and PCs. KleidiAI and software integrations support adoption; no incremental SaaS stream is assumed. Zena CSS, safety and real-time processing support automotive and robotics; long qualification cycles and unspecified robotics volumes constrain the near-term earnings bridge. [results, physical]

Meta confirms multigeneration CPU co-development. AWS confirms strong Graviton commitments, supporting the IP route rather than proving purchases of Arm-designed CPUs. OCI availability is planned for fall 2027. Customer-specific agreement economics remain undisclosed. Software and OEM ecosystem membership is not a chip purchase order. [meta, aws, oracle]

## FY2031 earnings sensitivities

Four unweighted scenarios, not consensus, company GAAP guidance or price targets. IP gross margin 99%; chip margins 35/45/50/55%. Non-SBC development/SG&A $5.6B in the first three cases and $6.3B in strong execution; SBC $2.5B/$3B plus employer payroll taxes $0.30B/$0.35B and acquisition costs $0.05B. Diluted shares 1.20B/1.25B, tax 15%, no interest or equity gains. Expenses are retained once. Manufacturing/packaging are in gross margin; ordinary R&D is in opex. [scenario assumptions]

| Scenario | IP revenue | Chip revenue | Total revenue | GAAP-style EPS | P/E at $152.20 | March 2031 price at assumed 30x GAAP | Annual price return |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Delayed chip ramp | $8B | $5B | $13B | $0.86 | 176.1x | $26 | -33.4% |
| Partial scale | $9B | $10B | $19B | $3.51 | 43.3x | $105 | -8.1% |
| Issuer revenue levels, modeled costs | $10B | $15B | $25B | $6.34 | 24.0x | $190 | 5.2% |
| Stronger scale and margins | $12B | $20B | $32B | $8.96 | 17.0x | $269 | 13.9% |

The $25B revenue case yields $8.36 adjusted-style EPS and $6.34 after restored costs under these assumptions. It is not a reconstruction of the issuer >$9 adjusted EPS target or the $10.40 vendor forecast. Those require different margins, costs, taxes, revenue or shares. At 30x GAAP EPS, a 12% annual price-return hurdle requires about $8.31 FY2031 EPS.

## What would strengthen or weaken the case

Strengthen: commercial deliveries in January–March 2027; accepted and recognized silicon revenue, expanding customer breadth; sustained growth in absolute datacenter royalties; chip gross margin rising on later generations; total recurring costs and diluted shares growing slower than gross profit.

Weaken: design/licensing growth without royalty conversion; chip revenue below the outlook; first-generation margins persisting; higher R&D/SBC; more customer overlap/cannibalization; supply or qualification delays. x86 and RISC-V remain competitive alternatives; customers also build their own chips. [annual]

The same partner can buy IP, use cloud instances and co-develop silicon; do not count its spending three times. Hardware mix lowers consolidated percentage gross margin but can still add profit dollars. Capital expenditure, purchase commitments, working capital, net cash and one-off tax effects remain outside this earnings model. SBC is noncash expense, not equal cash paid out; dilution is separately modeled without a second buyback charge.

Unknowns:

- Future diluted share count, SBC grant/vesting schedule, tax rates and full GAAP adjustments.
- TAM estimates are management expectations; no independent market sizing audit or calibrated scenario probabilities.
- Long-term analyst model assumptions are not available; this does not prove growth is absent from consensus.
