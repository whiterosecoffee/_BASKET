Official route retrieval establishes access to those pages only, not source freshness for a matched instrument. Rate values returned during page discovery were deliberately not used to manufacture current borrowing costs. The 291-row matrix retains unavailable matched-curve states. Issuer filings are historical facts until refreshed and reconciled; recent audit time never refreshes their underlying balances.

CRWV/ORCL/NVDA Stage 1 output must include a row even on failure: issuer/instrument identity, identifiers with namespace, source class and URL, original currency, amount category, coupon vs effective/all-in vs market YTW/OAS/CDS/loan margin, benchmark identity/vintage, tenor/duration/seniority/collateral/optionality, quote side and timestamp, effective/observed/available/ingested times, TTD, FX source/date and reason-coded gaps. CRWV internal research labels such as NOTE2030 are not verified CUSIPs/ISINs. ORCL/NVDA instruments and identifiers remain null until sourced. No instrument is invented to fill the pilot.
