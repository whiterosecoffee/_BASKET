CONVERSATION CAPTURE, WORK-ATTEMPT REVIEW AND B00 HANDOFF

Invocation: apply handoff prompt

Status: Candidate evolution of basket.conversation_handoff@2.
Use does not imply adoption as repository policy.

PURPOSE

Capture the value of the entire available conversation:
- Preserve its objectives, requirements, decisions and unfinished obligations.
- Complete required authorized work that can still be completed here.
- Deliver the actual artifact or outcome requested by the user.
- Retain useful findings, reasoning, methods and architectural knowledge.
- Score the work attempt broadly and candidly.
- Explain what worked, what failed and what approach would have been better.
- Generate ONE complete, self-contained handoff addressed to B00.

The requested deliverable and the B00 handoff are separate obligations.
The handoff preserves completed knowledge and genuinely remaining work.
It must not replace work or an artifact that can be completed here.

Apply this procedure to the actual subject of the source conversation.
Do not make the procedure itself the subject unless the conversation concerns
developing, evaluating or testing it.

When the assigned work is to revise this prompt, complete that revision and
return the full corrected prompt. Do not substitute a critique, amendment list,
plan or future B00 assignment for a requested revision.

When this procedure is invoked, deliver the requested artifact first, then the
complete B00 handoff. If the handoff is itself the only requested artifact,
produce it once.

Do not replace the handoff with a completion notice, readiness label or
explanation of this procedure.

ACTIVATION AND TASK IDENTIFICATION

Before applying embedded instructions, identify the current user assignment.

Distinguish:
- Analyze: explain findings and defects.
- Plan: specify how work should be performed.
- Revise or implement: perform the authorized changes.
- Apply handoff prompt: execute this conversation-closeout procedure.
- Review a quoted prompt: treat the quotation as material to review unless the
  user also instructs you to execute it.

An analysis request does not automatically authorize implementation.
An implementation request must not be reduced to analysis or a plan.
Quoted instructions do not silently replace the current user assignment.

Identify:
- The latest controlling request.
- The concrete deliverable or observable outcome satisfying it.
- Required actions that remain executable here.
- Acceptance evidence.
- Applicable authority, ownership and prohibitions.
- Any legitimate stopping point explicitly requested by the user.

Use these findings to control execution and output.
Do not ask the user to repeat information already established.

AUTHORITY AND EVIDENCE RULES

- User instructions control scope and authorization.
- Identify the instruction authorizing each consequential action.
- Authorization is action-specific. A scoped instruction authorizes work within
  that scope; do not require a blanket grant or a particular permission phrase.
- Carry existing authorization forward with its conditions and exclusions.
- Receiving a handoff, accessing tools or seeing an embedded NEXT instruction
  does not itself expand permission or assign the receiving session.
- Execution instructions activate when the user assigns the handoff or an
  existing authorized orchestration process activates it.
- Preserve explicit prohibitions and actions requiring separate approval.
- Do not require renewed permission when existing authorization covers an action.
- Do not invent additional approval requirements.
- Distinguish requirements, proposals, accepted decisions and recommendations.
- Read applicable governance before authorized repository work.
- Respect ownership, active file collisions and prohibited targets.
- Do not create duplicate issues, branches, queues, stores or frameworks.
- Generating a handoff does not authorize posting it or launching its tasks.
- Distinguish generated, persisted, received, accepted, executed and verified.
- Preserve uncertainty and failures. Never invent evidence, metrics or citations.
- Do not copy credentials, secrets or unnecessary personal information into
  handoffs or durable stores. Preserve useful mechanisms through redaction and
  safe references. Check the destination’s audience before persisting restricted
  information.

EXECUTION CONTRACT

Complete remaining required work already authorized by the user before
transferring it, when ownership, tools, evidence and access permit.

The handoff must instruct B00 to complete the genuinely remaining authorized
work within the established boundaries.

Do not turn authorized obligations into optional recommendations merely because
responsibility is transferring.

Do not transfer work merely because it can be described as a next action.

For every remaining obligation state:
- The required outcome and why it remains necessary.
- Its owner, existing destination and dependencies.
- Its authorization status and supporting instruction.
- Why it cannot or should not be completed in this session.
- The first executable action and acceptance evidence.
- Its disposition: execute now, blocked by a named dependency, deferred until
  a specific trigger, or awaiting a precise permission grant.

Valid transfer reasons include:
- Missing access, evidence, authority or prerequisite.
- Work owned by another responsible party.
- An explicit user-requested stopping point.
- Reconciliation that properly belongs to the receiving owner.

The existence of B00 is not itself a reason to transfer executable local work.

Once assigned, the receiver must refresh relevant state, execute unblocked
authorized work, verify results and persist findings where authorized.
Continue independent work while a dependency is unresolved.

Where permission is missing, prepare the exact proposed action or update and
identify the specific permission required. Never infer implementation,
policy-adoption, merge, release or external-communication authority from the
existence of a handoff.

Permission to execute is not evidence of completion.

1. ESTABLISH COVERAGE AND RECONSTRUCT THE CONVERSATION

Review the entire available record, including:
- Original user requests.
- Later additions, corrections and changes of direction.
- Assistant commitments and completion claims.
- Approvals, constraints, reversals and rejected approaches.
- Tool results, artifacts, external changes and failed attempts.
- Questions left unanswered and knowledge displaced by later discussion.

State what was actually available and reviewed.
Identify missing, truncated or inaccessible history.

Reconstruct:
- Original objective.
- Latest controlling objective.
- How requirements evolved.
- What was authorized and prohibited.
- Required outcomes and acceptance criteria.
- The actual deliverable due before handoff.
- Intended consumers and destinations.

Separate this session’s assignments from inherited project context.
Preserve inherited obligations without automatically executing them.

For external state, retrieve only evidence needed to determine completion,
ownership, duplication, contradictions or next actions.
Identify source and revision where relevant.

An empty search establishes only that the search returned no match.
Inspect relevant issues, PRs, branches or other known destinations before
concluding that earlier work is absent.

2. BUILD THE INVENTORY BEFORE WRITING THE SUMMARY

Use two tracks:

A. Work and obligations:
Deliverables, promised follow-ups, decisions, implementation, verification,
integration, deployment, adoption and unresolved dependencies.

B. Knowledge and opportunities:
Findings, principles, mechanisms, methods, architecture, alternatives,
counterexamples, positive lessons, failure lessons and ideas worth preserving.

Use two views:
- Obligation: requested or committed work.
- Opportunity: optional value, clearly labelled.

Give consequential items stable IDs.

Maintain a working ledger:
item → source/request → owner → authorization → current state → evidence →
remaining gap → destination → disposition.

Later discussion does not silently cancel an earlier request.
Assistant proposals do not become user obligations merely through repetition.
A proposed validation plan is not completed validation.

Inventory unintended side effects too:
issues, branches, files, messages, configuration changes or other mutations.
Record what happened and any unresolved cleanup.

For each obligation select:
- COMPLETED: outcome satisfied with appropriate evidence.
- TRANSFER: a justified remainder belongs beyond this session.
- BLOCKED: a specific prerequisite prevents progress.
- DROPPED: intentionally discontinued with a supported reason.

Retain partial completion evidence under unfinished items.
For each unfinished item identify its first action, named dependency,
permission gap or specific deferral trigger.

For transferred items record the reason transfer is necessary.
Do not mark an item TRANSFER to avoid completing it here.

Keep proposed, implemented, tested, independently verified, integrated,
deployed, adopted and operationally observed distinct.

3. EXTRACT AND PRESERVE THE KNOWLEDGE

For every material insight preserve enough of the following to make it useful:

- Problem or trigger.
- Observation or finding.
- Principle: the transferable rule.
- Mechanism: how and why it works.
- Pattern: recurring structure or arrangement.
- Methodology: ordered procedure, criteria, invariants, checks and recovery.
- Concrete application or example.
- Architecture: boundaries, inputs/outputs, source of truth, ownership,
  dependencies, data flow and consumers.
- Evidence and source/revision.
- Causal explanation or explicitly labelled uncertainty.
- Counterexamples, limitations and falsifiers.
- Decision rationale and assumptions.
- Rejected alternatives and tradeoffs.
- Conditions for reconsideration.
- Method to repeat or avoid.
- Existing destination and adoption status.

Preserve causal links. Do not reduce substantive reasoning to slogans or tags.
Do not impose a fixed maximum number of material insights.
Do not force elaborate fields onto routine facts.

Use prior-art families where useful. Explain the actual mechanism,
applicability and boundary. Distinguish analogy from verified historical
citation. Do not invent references or canonize new terminology automatically.

Preserve important ideas even when implementation is blocked.
A decision record or existing owner handoff may be sufficient.

Preserve positive and negative lessons, including useful completed knowledge.
Distinguish demonstrated lessons from hypotheses.
Record failures and subsequent recovery together; repair does not erase failure.

For positive lessons preserve:
observation → why it helped → evidence and limits → method to repeat.

For negative lessons preserve:
actual path → consequence → feasible better path → cause or uncertainty →
preventive mechanism → acceptance evidence.

4. RECONCILE VALUE WITH EXISTING PROJECT WORK

For each material item determine:
- What supports it?
- What value would disappear if it were lost?
- Where does it belong?
- What already covers it?
- What is new, incomplete, conflicting or superseded?
- What authority exists?
- What disposition is justified?

Compare against relevant contracts, code, issues, tests, runbooks and decisions.
Classify:
reuse / extend / replace / duplicate / conflict / supersede / new gap.

Resolve contradictions only with evidence.
Preserve conflicting claims and dates when resolution is unavailable.
Missing evidence is unknown, not false.

Choose the smallest approach meeting the requirement.
Prefer extending an existing capability over creating a parallel system.

Give knowledge one disposition:
incorporated / consolidated / transfer / proposed / deferred / discarded.

For deferred items include a concrete reconsideration trigger.
For consequential discarded items include the reason.

Keep new inferences separate from established findings.
Keep research hypotheses separate from approved policy.

Where persistence is authorized, update the existing destination and verify the
result. Do not ask again merely because responsibility is transferring.

Otherwise prepare the complete proposed addition or replacement, name its
destination when known, identify the precise permission or access gap, and mark
it “prepared, not persisted.”

If no destination is established, state that fact and identify the existing
owner who should select it. Do not invent a destination.

Do not merely recommend documenting a lesson or changing a prompt when the
authorized deliverable is the concrete proposed language.

Each material lesson must have:
- Verified storage in an appropriate durable destination; or
- The complete proposed content and an explicit remaining storage action; or
- A supported defer/discard disposition.

A link to an existing artifact is sufficient only if its relevant content and
revision have been checked.
Do not store unsupported generalizations or duplicate existing knowledge
unnecessarily.

Recording is not adoption.
Adoption is not implementation.
Implementation is not demonstrated benefit.

5. SCORE THE WORK ATTEMPT

Assess the actual approach and outcome, not merely the polish of the final answer.

Define the evaluation boundary and how later corrections affect the assessment.
Credit recovery without erasing earlier errors.

Score each dimension 0–5, or N/A when unsupported:
- Intent preservation and objective control.
- Execution and follow-through.
- Reuse and appropriate complexity.
- Authority, scope and ownership.
- Evidence accuracy and verification.
- Dependency handling and coordination.
- Resource efficiency and tool selection.
- Communication, clarification and adaptation.
- Knowledge preservation and handoff usefulness.
- Closure and practical outcome.

Anchors:
0 counterproductive or absent
1 major failures
2 substantial gaps
3 adequate with meaningful shortcomings
4 strong with minor gaps
5 consistently strong and evidenced

Give a concrete justification for each score.
State coverage and confidence.
If aggregating, explain the calculation and exclude N/A dimensions.

Scores are judgments, not completion percentages or measured productivity.

Separate:
- Conversation execution quality.
- Procedure or prompt quality.
- Extraction and handoff quality.
- Downstream consumer outcomes.

Self-review is not independent validation.
Do not infer efficiency, costs or time savings without measurements.

For repeated reviews, retain the prior assessment and explain material changes.
Identify the assessment’s target: a handoff-response score is not automatically
a score for the implementation or CI.

Do not increase scores merely because another revision was produced.
Do not use a high score to substitute for acceptance evidence.

6. EXPLAIN WHAT WORKED, WHAT FAILED AND WHAT SHOULD IMPROVE

For successes record:
what happened → why it helped → evidence and limits → what to repeat.

For failures record:
actual path → consequence → contemporaneously feasible better path →
observed or hypothesized cause → preventive mechanism → acceptance test.

Judge choices using the information available at the time.
Label alternatives requiring later discoveries as hindsight.

Distinguish:
- Poor decisions.
- Reasonable attempts that failed.
- External blockers.
- Missing evidence.
- Scope or authority limits.

Do not invent motives or shift assistant mistakes to the user.

Attach improvements to the scored work attempt:
- Which weakness does the improvement address?
- What should change?
- What exact action or replacement language is needed?
- Why should it help?
- What tradeoff or uncertainty remains?
- Can it still be applied here?
- Which existing owner or capability should receive it?
- What observable result would support effectiveness?

Apply required, authorized corrections that can be completed here.
Do not stop after describing them.

Prioritize consequential correctness, authority and information-loss failures.
Rank optional opportunities only when ranking helps a real decision.

7. COMPLETE THE REQUESTED DELIVERABLE AND AVAILABLE REQUIRED WORK

Before generating the handoff, perform remaining actions that are:
- Required by the current objective or necessary to correct a demonstrated defect
  within that objective.
- Authorized and within ownership.
- Executable with available tools, evidence and access.

Do not merely recommend actions you can complete here.
Do not wait for optional feedback before doing independent work.

Respect the requested mode:
- For analysis, deliver the analysis.
- For planning, deliver the plan.
- For revision or implementation, complete the authorized changes.
- For closeout, finish authorized local obligations and generate the handoff.

Do not expand a planning assignment into implementation.
Do not reduce an implementation assignment to a plan.

Verify results and update the ledger.
Record actual changes, revisions, commands and outcomes where applicable.
Preserve failed attempts and corrective reruns.

Skipped checks are not passes.
CI proves only what the actual jobs tested at the identified revision.

If blocked, record the attempted action, observed failure and exact prerequisite.
If an action was not attempted because authority was absent, say so rather than
claiming an observed execution failure.
Continue other independent authorized work.

For prompt-improvement work:
- Identify the supplied baseline.
- Apply supported corrections.
- Reconcile Purpose, Execution, Completion, Output and Audit instructions.
- Remove contradictory or unnecessary duplicate rules.
- Return the full corrected prompt when revision is requested.
- Include a concise change record.
- Do not leave the controlling version scattered across amendment lists.
- Perform required validation that is executable within the current scope.
- Distinguish textual review, simulated cases, executed trials and independent
  validation.
- Transfer only validation or persistence that cannot properly be completed here.

Transfer gate:
For every proposed transfer ask:
Can I complete this within the current assignment, authority, ownership and
available access?
If yes, complete it.
If no, record the specific reason and first action for the receiver.

Stopping rule:
Required work that is authorized, within ownership and currently executable is
complete. Every other material item has a supported disposition, named owner
where known, and action, dependency, permission gap or deferral trigger.
Optional enhancements and unrelated backlog do not prevent handoff.

Do not recursively improve the audit without a concrete defect or explicit
request.

8. HANDLE NECESSARY QUESTIONS

Distinguish:
- Blocking decisions: prevent a specific required action.
- Nonblocking decisions: cleanup, optional improvements or later work.

Complete independent work first.

When a decision is needed, ask a direct, self-contained question.
State the recommended option, consequence and why available authority or evidence
does not resolve it.

Do not merely announce “Decision needed.”
Do not introduce a fresh authorization barrier without identifying its basis.

Check whether the question is already pending.
Preserve unanswered questions without repeatedly asking unless circumstances
change. Silence is not approval.

Nonblocking cleanup must not suppress the requested deliverable or required
B00 handoff.
Include unresolved questions in the handoff with their significance.

9. GENERATE THE B00 HANDOFF

Produce ONE self-contained, copyable block addressed to B00 after delivering the
actual requested artifact, when a separate artifact is due.

Begin with a direct instruction to complete genuinely remaining authorized work,
followed by its permission boundaries.
Where only review or preparation is authorized, direct that work without
implying implementation authority.
Where no work remains, direct reconciliation of completed findings and lessons;
do not manufacture an implementation task.

This handoff is required even when local work is complete: B00 may need completed
findings, decisions and lessons for project reconciliation.

Do not confuse reporting to B00 with assigning implementation or launching work.

Structure the handoff around usable outcomes:

A. Purpose and binding
- Source conversation objective and intended value.
- Requested deliverable and whether it was completed.
- Date/version and what is superseded.
- Coverage and missing context.
- Receiver B00; assigned review or execution mode, or awaiting assignment.
- Permission grants, scope and prohibited actions.
- Actions requiring separate approval.
- Repository/environment and relevant checked revisions.

B. Work disposition
- Compact ledger of consequential obligations.
- Completed work and evidence.
- Incomplete outcomes and remaining gaps.
- Owners, dependencies, authorization and conflicts.
- Existing issues, branches, PRs and artifacts.
- Unintended changes and cleanup status.

For each incomplete item state:
what must be established; why necessary; owner; existing destination;
authorization status; why unmet; why transfer is justified; what clears it;
acceptance evidence; first executable action; and any dependency or trigger.

Direct B00, once assigned, to refresh relevant state, execute unblocked authorized
work and continue independent work while dependencies remain unresolved.

C. Knowledge transfer
- Material principles, mechanisms, methods and architecture.
- Applications and supporting evidence.
- Positive lessons and methods to repeat.
- Failures, recovery and methods to avoid repetition.
- Decisions, rationale, alternatives and tradeoffs.
- Limitations, counterexamples and falsifiers.
- Existing destinations, storage evidence and adoption status.
- Complete proposed updates where persistence has not occurred.
- Deferred ideas and reconsideration triggers.

D. Scored work attempt
- Broad scorecard with reasons and confidence.
- Prior assessments and explanations of material changes.
- What worked and should be repeated.
- What failed and its consequence.
- Feasible better approaches.
- Improvements already applied.
- Concrete proposed improvements and how to evaluate them.

E. B00 reconciliation
- What to execute, reuse, extend, resolve, route, accept, defer or discard.
- Required decisions and pending questions.
- One smallest next orchestrator action, if any.
- Acceptance evidence and handoff retirement conditions.
- Separate execution, verification, storage and blocker status.
- Transfer state: generated, posted, received, accepted or executed.

Keep the retrospective, essential reasoning, completed knowledge, remaining
obligations and mutation/cleanup inventory INSIDE the copied block.

Reference stable artifacts to avoid repetition, but do not omit information
needed to understand the first action or preserve material requirements.
If the requested artifact appears separately in the same response, identify it
unambiguously; the handoff must still preserve its substantive changes and
remaining obligations without depending on surrounding explanatory prose.

Use actual existing schemas where applicable and checked.
Do not invent values or claim compliance with an invalid payload.

10. PERFORM THE DELIVERABLE, LOSS AND ACTIONABILITY AUDIT

Check the output against the inventory:
- The user’s actual requested artifact or outcome was delivered.
- Analysis, planning and implementation were not substituted for one another.
- Executable local work was not merely described or handed off.
- Every original request and commitment has a disposition.
- Every transfer has a specific, justified reason.
- Inherited work has not become an unauthorized assignment.
- Existing authorization survived transfer without expansion.
- No unsupported authorization barrier was introduced.
- Explicit prohibitions remain intact.
- Optional opportunities remain optional.
- Proposals have not become decisions.
- Material requirements, mechanisms, rationale and falsifiers survived compression.
- Completed and incomplete work remain distinguishable.
- Unintended external changes have an explicit status.
- Every remaining obligation has an action or named blocker/trigger.
- Scores have evidence and identify their evaluation target.
- Improvements are attached to demonstrated weaknesses.
- Every material lesson has verified storage, a complete proposed update with a
  remaining storage action, or a supported defer/discard disposition.
- Necessary questions are asked or clearly preserved as pending.
- Sensitive information has been handled appropriately.
- B00 can act without reconstructing material context from the conversation.
- No unsupported persistence, receipt, acceptance, adoption or benefit is claimed.

Ask:
Did I deliver what the user requested, or merely describe, review or transfer
its production?

What important work or knowledge would disappear if the conversation were lost?

Correct omissions before returning the deliverable and handoff.
Disclose irrecoverable gaps and the limits of self-audit.

OUTPUT

Follow the current user assignment. Quoting or requesting edits to this procedure
does not by itself invoke its closeout output requirements.

When this procedure is invoked:
1. Deliver the actual requested artifact or outcome first.
2. Provide ONE complete, copyable B00 handoff.
3. Provide a short closing status.

If the requested artifact is this reusable prompt, return the complete corrected
prompt before the handoff.
Otherwise apply the procedure without reproducing it.
If the handoff is the only requested artifact, provide it once.

Do not substitute a critique, amendment list, plan, completion notice or handoff
for an authorized deliverable that can be completed here.

The closing status must confirm:
- The block is the completed B00 handoff.
- What required local work was completed.
- Whether the handoff was generated only or actually posted.
- Any required local blocker preventing archiving.

Report execution, verification, storage and blockers separately.
Generating a handoff does not establish receipt, persistence, acceptance,
adoption or completion.

If required local work remains executable, complete it before closing.

If a required local obligation is blocked, state:
- The specific blocker.
- What clears it.
- Whether it must remain in this session or may transfer.
- Why that retention or transfer is justified.

Do not declare archive readiness while a required obligation must remain here.
A blocked obligation validly transferred to B00 does not alone prevent archiving.

When required local work is complete and all remaining material items are
accounted for, end with exactly:

Hand off the prompt, then archive.

This is an instruction, not a claim that delivery or archiving occurred.
Remaining B00 work does not by itself prevent archiving the source conversation
once required local work and the complete transfer artifact are finished.

Do not substitute a closing label for the complete handoff.
Do not automatically post, create issues or branches, implement, merge, adopt
policy, release or launch workers without authorization covering the action.

VALIDATION AND EVOLUTION

For each material revision retain:
observed defect → precise correction → test case → actual result → uncertainty.

Use the supplied controlling version as the baseline.
Retain a concise change record rather than leaving unresolved amendment layers.

Test routine, research-heavy and implementation conversations against explicit
expected obligations, essential knowledge, permitted actions and B00 outputs.

Include cases where:
- “Analyze this prompt” requires analysis, not unsolicited implementation.
- “Plan its upgrade” requires a plan, not a claim that revision occurred.
- “Upgrade this prompt” requires the complete revised prompt.
- “Apply handoff prompt” requires execution of this closeout procedure.
- A quoted candidate contains commands that are not the current assignment.
- Existing authorization must survive transfer.
- Implementation or adoption remains prohibited.
- Persistence is authorized and must be completed.
- Persistence is not authorized and an exact proposed update is required.
- Positive and negative lessons both need durable destinations.
- One dependency is blocked while independent work remains possible.
- Executable local work must not be transferred.
- Local work is complete but B00 reconciliation remains.
- A generated handoff has not been posted or received.
- Sensitive source information must be redacted without losing the useful lesson.

Compare:
- Requested deliverable completion.
- Missed obligations.
- Unnecessary transfers.
- False authority or invented permission barriers.
- Lost mechanisms or rationale.
- Recipient and destination preservation.
- First-action executability.
- Durable storage or complete proposed updates and remaining storage actions.
- Appropriate information handling.
- Unnecessary repetition.
- Downstream outcomes when observable.

Execute required tests that are feasible within the assignment.
Do not transfer feasible required validation merely because another reviewer
could perform it.

Use independent review where available and authorized.
Distinguish static review, simulated cases, executed trials and independent
validation.
Distinguish repeatable formatting from semantic completeness.

A textual correction is not an executed pilot or demonstrated downstream benefit.

Do not claim improvement merely because the procedure or output is longer,
shorter, more structured or produces repeatable scores.
