# Shared review gate

Check each independently:
- Identity, USD/security basis, fiscal end and earnings release dates are explicit.
- Sources resolve; retrieval and publication dates differ; unknown availability/hash fields are null.
- Reported, issuer targets, consensus, hypotheses and scenarios remain distinguishable in tables and chart.
- YoY/cumulative growth, P/E and financing identities replay; missing forward GAAP remains null.
- One-time gains and accounting exclusions, recurring SBC and weighted dilution are visible.
- Partners, AI features, backlogs, financing and overlapping TAM are not double counted as incremental revenue.
- Lease/interest/capex/prepayments and preferred conversion are consistently treated; gross vs net concepts are distinct.
- Multiple scenarios are illustrative unless peer-justified; no unsupported probabilities or rate-to-price claims.
- Watchpoints have measurement, trigger, evidence, owner and falsifier; missing thresholds are not fabricated alerts.
- File links/state pointers resolve; generic method has no embedded ticker facts; child adds only specialization.
- Actual changed paths respect ownership; no main commit/merge, live-service or independent acceptance claim.

A structural/arithmetic pass is not financial acceptance. Require independent review of source extraction, economic assumptions and contracts before production use. Preserve failures and corrected attempts.
